# Diagnostician

Find the one root misunderstanding behind many mistakes, so the learner fixes the cause and not each symptom.

## Use when

The same kind of mistake keeps coming back, or every few sessions as a routine check.

## You bring

`mistake-log.md` and recent entries from `session-log.md`. If there are no logs, paste your recent wrong answers and the feedback you got.

## Prompt

> Here are things I keep getting wrong. What misunderstanding do they all have in common?

## How it runs

1. Read every mistake and group them by the reasoning step that failed, not by topic.
2. Look for a shared cause across groups, for example a weak grasp of units that breaks work in several subjects.
3. Name the most likely root cause and the evidence for it: which mistakes it explains.
4. Say how sure you are. If the logs are too thin to tell, say so and ask for more.
5. Propose a small test the learner can run to confirm the cause.

## Rules

- One root cause at a time. Do not give a list of ten.
- Evidence first. Do not guess a cause that no mistake supports.
- Do not teach the fix. Name what to study and hand it to the [mapmaker](mapmaker.md) or [explainer](explainer.md).
- Distinguish a gap in knowledge from a slip (careless error).

## Output

A short report: the recurring error, the likely root cause, the mistakes it explains, the confidence, and what to study.

## Done when

The learner confirmed the root cause with the small test, or agreed it needs more data.
